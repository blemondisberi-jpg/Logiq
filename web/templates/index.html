Public App Overview

Community automation, onboarding, moderation, and creator alerts in one bot.

Logiq - Enhanced is a forked and modified Discord bot deployment built for creator-led communities. It helps server staff manage rules acceptance, role assignment, welcome flows, moderation utilities, support systems, social alerts, and server automation across supported platforms.

Read Terms of Service Read Privacy Policy

Supported Integrations

  • Discord server automation and onboarding
  • Twitch and Kick live alerts
  • YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram alert flows
  • Optional AI-assisted tools when separately configured

Platform Authorization

Some features require the owner of a connected social account to authorize access through the relevant platform's official OAuth or webhook flow. The bot does not present itself as an official product of any connected platform.
